# Decisions

Keep only decisions that prevent future agents from reopening a settled question. Code-level detail
that is easy to discover does not belong here.

## Decision template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title

- **Status:** accepted | superseded
- **Context:** why a choice was necessary
- **Decision:** what was selected
- **Consequences:** constraints and tradeoffs created
- **Evidence:** issue, test, commit, or implementation path
